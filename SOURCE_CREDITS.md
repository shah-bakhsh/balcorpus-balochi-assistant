# Training source acknowledgements

The commercial source pool contains CC BY 4.0 materials. This release does not include the underlying corpus. Conservative extraction/normalization preserved original orthography; no new native-language approval is claimed.

- WOWA Balochi narratives: Maryam Nourzaei, Geoffrey Haig and the original collectors named in the accompanying citations. Original licence and citations are preserved below.
- Global Digital Library publisher descriptions: Pakistan Reading Project / USAID, not full story text.
- Beyond the Wall: Danish Dagh, Noroz Hayat, Susan Weldon, Carina Jahani. https://journals.uu.se/orientaliasuecana/article/view/694
- Alas: Azgar Lal, Taj Baloch, Carina Jahani. https://journals.uu.se/orientaliasuecana/article/view/644
